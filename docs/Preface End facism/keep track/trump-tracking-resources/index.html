Master Directory: Trump Administration Trackers (2025–2026)

Day-by-Day Logs, Departmental/DOGE Actions, Litigation, and Lethal Force / Mortality Trackers

1. Day-by-Day Chronological Logs & General Action Trackers​

  1. What The Fuck Just Happened Today? (WTFJHT) — https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com
    Strict day-by-day weekday log ("Day 1, Day 2...") summarizing the top executive, departmental, and legislative events with primary source links.
  2. Trump Action Tracker (Prof. Christina Pagel, UCL) — https://www.trumpactiontracker.info
    Searchable database logging executive actions, directives, and statements since Jan 20, 2025, categorized by domains of authoritarianism.
  3. Trump Tyranny Tracker (Olga Lautman) — https://trumptyrannytracker.substack.com
    Daily chronological log tracking democratic backsliding, DOJ/intelligence weaponization, and civil rights rollbacks.
  4. The Trump Tracker — https://thetrumptracker.com
    Evaluates and sources administration actions against fixed severity rubrics (abuse of power, rule of law, corruption).
  5. r/Keep_Track — https://www.reddit.com/r/Keep_Track/
    Crowd-sourced archival community documenting executive actions, judicial defiance, and departmental corruption.
  6. The Federal Register (and Public REST API) — https://www.federalregister.gov (API: https://www.federalregister.gov/api/v1/documents.json)
    Official daily government log of every Executive Order, Presidential Memorandum, Proclamation, and agency rule.
  7. White House Presidential Actions Archive — https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/
    Primary-source feed of signed presidential orders, memoranda, and proclamations.
  8. Ballotpedia Presidential Actions Tracker — https://ballotpedia.org
    Running quantitative tally and chronological subject index of executive orders, memoranda, and proclamations.

2. Project 2025 & Blueprint Alignment Trackers​

  1. Center for Progressive Reform & Governing for Impact – Project 2025 Executive Action Tracker — https://progressivereform.org
    Cross-references administration regulatory and executive actions directly against Project 2025 proposals.
  2. Project 2025 Observer — https://project2025.observer
    Community-driven tracker monitoring the rollout and implementation status of specific Project 2025 goals.

3. Departmental, DOGE, Civil Service & Corporate Favoritism Trackers​

  1. OpenFeds — https://openfeds.org
    Tracks the 280,000–300,000+ federal civilian positions eliminated via DOGE, "Fork in the Road" deferred resignations, and RIFs.
  2. Partnership for Public Service — https://ourpublicservice.org
    Tracks civil service purges, probationary firings, Schedule F reclassifications, and agency capacity loss.
  3. Brookings Institution – Treasury Outlay Tracker & Regulatory Tracker — https://www.brookings.edu
    Monitors daily Treasury cash flows to detect unannounced funding freezes/impoundments and tracks agency deregulation.
  4. DOGE Official Dashboard — https://doge.gov
    Official administration claims of contract/lease cancellations and layoffs (used alongside GAO reports to audit actual vs. claimed figures).
  5. Public Citizen – Corporate Enforcement Tracker — https://www.citizen.org
    Tracks federal investigations and lawsuits against corporations (DOJ, SEC, CFPB, FTC, EPA, DOL) dropped, frozen, or settled by the administration.
  6. Columbia Law School (Sabin Center) & CSLDF – Silencing Science Tracker — https://climate.law.columbia.edu/Silencing-Science-Tracker
    Logs agency censorship of scientists, scrubbed federal websites/datasets, canceled research grants, and disbanded advisory boards.
  7. Columbia Law School (Sabin Center) – Climate Backtracker — https://climate.law.columbia.edu
    Catalogs every action by the EPA, Interior, and Energy departments to dismantle climate and environmental regulations.
  8. KFF (Kaiser Family Foundation) – Health Policy Tracker — https://www.kff.org & https://kffhealthnews.org
    Tracks HHS, CDC, FDA, and CMS rollbacks under RFK Jr., including vaccine schedule changes, CDC division cuts (NIOSH, HIV/STD), and Medicaid shifts.
  9. STAT News (HHS & Public Health Coverage) — https://www.statnews.com
    Investigative tracking of biomedical research cuts (NIH), FDA deregulation, and infectious disease outbreaks.
  10. American Oversight — https://www.americanoversight.org
    FOIA-driven watchdog tracking departmental corruption, ethics violations, and political appointees.
  11. CREW (Citizens for Responsibility and Ethics in Washington) — https://www.citizensforethics.org
    Tracks conflicts of interest, self-enrichment, ethics breaches, and agency politicization.
  12. POGO (Project On Government Oversight) — https://www.pogo.org
    Tracks inspector general firings, whistleblower retaliation, and federal oversight rollbacks.

4. Litigation & Legal Challenge Trackers​

  1. Lawfare – Trump Administration Litigation Tracker — https://www.lawfaremedia.org
    Comprehensive database of active federal lawsuits, injunctions, and court rulings across national security, impoundment, and domestic deployments.
  2. Just Security – Litigation Tracker — https://www.justsecurity.org
    Chronological tracker of major constitutional and statutory lawsuits against executive actions.
  3. Democracy Forward ( & Democracy 2025) — https://democracyforward.org & https://www.democracy2025.org
    Tracks legal challenges and court orders defending civil service protections, public health, and administrative law.
  4. National LGBTQ+ Bar Association – Anti-LGBTQ+ EO Litigation Tracker — https://lgbtqbar.org
    Tracks lawsuits challenging executive orders targeting gender identity, civil rights enforcement, and military policy.
  5. ACLU Litigation & Civil Rights Trackers — https://www.aclu.org
    Tracks court challenges across immigration enforcement, domestic deployments, free speech, and reproductive rights.
  6. Major Law Firm Regulatory & EO Trackers
    Corporate legal databases tracking second-term executive orders and agency shifts:
    • Skadden: https://www.skadden.com
    • Mayer Brown: https://www.mayerbrown.com
    • Holland & Knight: https://www.hklaw.com
    • Morgan Lewis: https://www.morganlewis.com
    • Sheppard Mullin: https://www.sheppard.com

5. Trackers of Killings, Lethal Operations & Preventable Mortality​

  1. Airwars – US Military Strikes & Civilian Harm Tracker — https://airwars.org
    Tracks civilian casualties from US bombing campaigns in Yemen (Operation Rough Rider, 224+ civilians killed), Somalia, Iraq/Syria, Nigeria, and Iran, plus Pentagon civilian harm admissions.
  2. New America – Counterterrorism & Drone Wars Tracker — https://www.newamerica.org
    Tracks US drone and airstrike operations and casualties in Somalia (AFRICOM) and Yemen.
  3. ACLED (Armed Conflict Location & Event Data) — https://acleddata.com
    Global conflict event database tracking US military strikes, political violence, and demonstrations.
  4. InSight Crime & WOLA (Washington Office on Latin America) — https://insightcrime.org & https://www.wola.org
    Track "Operation Southern Spear" and the 70–80+ extrajudicial US military airstrikes on alleged "narco-terrorist" boats in the Caribbean and Eastern Pacific (240+ people killed).
  5. Impact Counter (Dr. Brooke Nichols, Boston University School of Public Health) — https://www.impactcounter.com
    Epidemiological dashboard tracking cumulative preventable adult and child deaths globally resulting from the USAID and PEPFAR foreign aid freezes (HIV/AIDS, TB, malaria, malnutrition).
  6. TRAC Immigration (Syracuse University) — https://tracreports.org
    Tracks daily ICE detention populations (60,000–68,000+), facility conditions, and shows >70% of detainees have no criminal convictions.
  7. Human Rights Watch (HRW) – "Dying in Detention" & Immigration Reports — https://www.hrw.org
    Tracks ICE in-custody deaths, medical neglect, and the doubling of detention mortality rates.
  8. AILA (American Immigration Lawyers Association) – ICE Death Logs — https://www.aila.org
    Running log of ICE in-custody death notices, detention oversight rollbacks, and compliance failures.
  9. National Immigration Project (NIPNLG) & American Immigration Council — https://nipnlg.org & https://www.americanimmigrationcouncil.org
    Track detention fatalities, "hidden deaths" (releasing comatose detainees right before death to evade reporting), and enforcement raids.
  10. Southern Border Communities Coalition (SBCC), Mapping Police Violence, & L.A. TACO Raid Tracker — https://www.southernborder.org / https://mappingpoliceviolence.org / https://lataco.com
    Track fatal shootings and lethal use-of-force incidents by ICE, CBP, and deputized forces during community raids and border operations.
  11. ProPublica ("Life of the Mother") & Guttmacher Institute — https://www.propublica.org & https://www.guttmacher.org
    Track preventable maternal deaths from denied emergency pregnancy care following the rollback of federal EMTALA protections.
  12. Death Penalty Information Center (DPIC) — https://deathpenaltyinfo.org
    Tracks Trump's Day-1 death penalty EO, DOJ lifting of the execution moratorium, firing squad authorizations, and military execution orders.
