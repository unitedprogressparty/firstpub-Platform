3. Prioritized Five-Tier Reversal Playbook for this Domain​

  • Tier 1 (Day-One Executive Orders): Rescind all identified presidential directives, restore agency statutory mandates, and cease unlawful enforcement procedures.
  • Tier 2 (Notice-and-Comment APA Rulemakings): Initiate formal emergency rulemakings to restore pre-November 2024 regulatory baselines and public protections.
  • Tier 3 (Judicial Consent Decrees): Settle pending litigation, confess error on unlawful appellate positions, and agree to binding injunctive decrees.
  • Tier 4 (Enforcement & Restitution): Re-open dismissed enforcement matters, audit dropped penalties, and pursue full statutory restitution.
  • Tier 5 (Institutional Rebuild & Reinstatement): Restore civil service protections, re-hire purged experts, and provide backpay compensation.
