Presidential Transition Blueprint: Day-One Omnibus Executive Orders

Operational Status: Ready-to-Sign Draft Administrative Directives
Target Execution: 12:01 PM, January 20, Inauguration Day
Legal Framework: Article II Constitutional Authority, 5 U.S.C. § 3301, 5 U.S.C. § 7301
Derived From: damage_ledger.db (8,273 canonical events; Tier 1 Reversal Inventory)

Executive Order 1: Restoring the Merit Civil Service and Revoking Unlawful Workforce Dismantling​

Section 1. Policy and Findings.​

A non-partisan, merit-based career civil service is the bedrock of democratic stability, constitutional adherence, and the faithful execution of laws. The actions of the preceding administration—specifically the arbitrary reclassification of tens of thousands of civil servants under Schedule F, coercive reduction-in-force (RIF) quotas mandated by the Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE), and the abrupt cancellation of essential facility leases and operational contracts—inflicted catastrophic harm on federal agency capacity, resulting in the unlawful removal or constructive discharge of an estimated 721,850 career personnel.

Section 2. Immediate Revocations.​

(a) Any Executive Order, Presidential Memorandum, or executive directive issued between November 5, 2024, and January 20, 2029, creating or reimplementing "Schedule F," "Schedule Policy Exempt," or any non-statutory excepted service classification for career civil servants is hereby REVOKED IN FULL.
(b) All directives, quotas, workforce reduction targets, and operational charters issued by or under the auspices of the entity known as the "Department of Government Efficiency" (DOGE) are hereby TERMINATED AND DECLARED NULL AND VOID.

Section 3. Civil Service Reinstatement and Restitution Pipeline.​

(a) The Director of the Office of Personnel Management (OPM), in consultation with the heads of executive departments and agencies, shall within 14 days establish the Emergency Civil Service Reinstatement Clearinghouse.
(b) Any career employee separated, reassigned, constructively discharged, or coerced into resignation under Schedule F or DOGE reduction quotas shall be entitled to expedited, non-competitive reinstatement with full seniority, backpay, restoration of retirement annuity credits, and accrued benefits under 5 U.S.C. § 5596 (Back Pay Act).
(c) The Federal Labor Relations Authority (FLRA) and Merit Systems Protection Board (MSPB) are directed to stay and dismiss all pending agency petitions seeking to decertify federal employee bargaining units or abrogate collective bargaining agreements.

Executive Order 2: Rescinding Second-Term Presidential Directives and Re-establishing Environmental, Public Health, and Climate Baselines​

Section 1. Comprehensive Rescission of Executive Actions.​

All Executive Orders, Presidential Memoranda, Proclamations, and Determinations issued between November 5, 2024, and January 20, 2029, that weaken, restrict, or purport to suspend statutory environmental, energy conservation, consumer protection, or civil rights standards are hereby REVOKED.

Section 2. Restoring Scientific Integrity and Advisory Panels.​

(a) The Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), the Secretary of Health and Human Services (HHS), and the Secretary of the Interior shall immediately reinstate all statutory Federal Advisory Committee Act (FACA) scientific review panels dissolved or dismissed during the second Trump term.
(b) All agency heads shall rescind guidelines restricting the use of peer-reviewed epidemiological, toxicological, and climate science in administrative decision-making.

Section 3. Public Lands and Fossil Fuel Leasing Moratorium.​

(a) The Secretary of the Interior shall immediately suspend all non-competitive mineral leasing, offshore drilling authorizations, and categorical exclusions under the National Environmental Policy Act (NEPA) granted between November 5, 2024, and January 20, 2029, pending a comprehensive 90-day environmental and public interest review.
(b) The Secretary of Energy shall reinstate the public interest review criteria for Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG) export authorizations to non-Free Trade Agreement nations.

Executive Order 3: Restoring Due Process, Rescinding Lethal Pursuit Protocols, and Transitioning Away from For-Profit Incarceration​

Section 1. Revocation of Permissive Deadly Force and Pursuit Standards.​

(a) The Secretary of Homeland Security shall, within 24 hours of this order, rescind all Department of Homeland Security (DHS), U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP), and U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) vehicle pursuit and use-of-force policy modifications that permitted high-speed tactical maneuvers in populated zones or relaxed firearm discharge standards.
(b) Continuous-recording body-worn camera (BWC) mandates and dashboard camera protocols shall be strictly reinstated across all operational encounters.

Section 2. Termination of Private Detention Facilities.​

(a) The Attorney General and the Secretary of Homeland Security shall not renew any contract with private, for-profit detention operators (including GEO Group and CoreCivic) for the operation of civil immigration or federal correctional facilities.
(b) DHS is directed to transition detained populations to community-based, non-carceral Alternatives to Detention (ATD) under intensive case management, prioritizing the closure of facilities with documented records of medical neglect and in-custody deaths.

Section 3. Cessation of Alien Enemies Act Invocations.​

Any invocation or operational enforcement of the Alien Enemies Act of 1798 (50 U.S.C. §§ 21–24) against civilian migrant populations without individual Article III judicial due process is hereby TERMINATED AND FORBIDDEN.

Executive Order 4: Protecting Emergency Reproductive Healthcare Under EMTALA and Restoring Biomedical Oversight​

Section 1. Emergency Medical Treatment and Labor Act (EMTALA) Affirmation.​

(a) The Secretary of Health and Human Services shall immediately reissue binding federal guidance to all hospitals participating in Medicare, confirming that under EMTALA (42 U.S.C. § 1395dd), if a physician determines that abortion care is necessary to stabilize an emergency medical condition, federal statutory law strictly preempts any state-level abortion restriction or criminal statute.
(b) The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) shall vigorously enforce EMTALA compliance, initiating immediate investigations and withholding Medicare provider agreements from facilities that turn away pregnant patients in medical distress.

Section 2. Protection of Medication Abortion and Biomedical Approvals.​

(a) The Commissioner of Food and Drugs (FDA) shall re-affirm the full scientific approvals, distribution protocols, and telehealth dispensing regulations for mifepristone and misoprostol, precluding politically motivated restrictions on FDA-approved pharmaceuticals.
(b) The Director of the National Institutes of Health (NIH) shall immediately lift any funding freeze, grant termination, or research restriction imposed on biomedical and genomic research programs.

Executive Order 5: Nullifying Corrupt Quashed Enforcement, Recovering Unpaid Penalties, and Establishing Ethics Special Counsels​

Section 1. Audit of Quashed Corporate Enforcement Actions.​

(a) There is hereby established within the Department of Justice the Corporate Enforcement Restitution Task Force, chaired by the Deputy Attorney General and comprising representatives from the SEC, CFPB, FTC, and EPA.
(b) The Task Force shall review every civil or criminal enforcement action, consent decree modification, or penalty reduction executed between November 5, 2024, and January 20, 2029, where the affected corporate entity, executives, or affiliated PACs donated to political committees or inauguration funds associated with the preceding administration.
(c) The Task Force is directed to refile charges within applicable statutes of limitations and aggressively pursue the collection of the $1,325,053,017.00 in uncollected penalties, fines, and restitution documented in the Trump Damage Ledger.

Section 2. Ethics Special Counsel and Pardon Audits.​

(a) The Attorney General shall appoint a Special Counsel for Government Integrity to examine corrupt transactions, improper interference in ongoing criminal prosecutions, and quid-pro-quo arrangements related to executive pardons and commutations.
(b) All federal executive branch ethics agreements, divestment pledges, and anti-nepotism rules under 5 U.S.C. § 3110 are restored to full operational effect.
