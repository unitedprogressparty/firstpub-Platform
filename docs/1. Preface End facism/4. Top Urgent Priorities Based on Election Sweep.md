# Top Urgent Priorities Based on an Election Sweep

An electoral sweep is a perishable mandate. Without immediate structural power reforms executed within the first hours of taking power, progressive majorities are systematically paralyzed by procedural vetoes, a rogue judiciary, leadership complacency, and culture-war traps.

To safeguard democracy, dismantle minority rule, and guarantee that the will of the majority translates into law, the incoming coalition must execute these **four urgent priorities** immediately upon taking office, while adhering strictly to realistic tactical sequencing.

---

## 1. End the Senate Filibuster (Immediate Procedural Disarmament)

* **The Problem:** The 60-vote legislative filibuster (Senate Rule XXII) allows a reactionary senatorial minority representing a fraction of the population to veto all transformative legislation—blocking voting rights, labor protections, healthcare, and economic relief.
* **Immediate Action:** On the very first day of the new legislative session, invoke the simple-majority rule change to permanently abolish the legislative filibuster for all bills and appointments.
* **Core Mandate:** Restore majority rule so that a swept Congress can govern effectively.
* **Platform Details:** See [**Legislative Reform: End the Filibuster**](../4.%20Domestic%20Agenda/4.%20Political%20Reform/4.%20Legislative.md#a-end-the-filibuster).

---

## 2. Install Aggressive, Reform-Minded Leadership

* **The Problem:** Decades of status-quo, risk-averse congressional and party leadership have normalized premature concession, negotiated away essential working-class benefits, and failed to fight creeping authoritarianism with the urgency required.
* **Immediate Action:** Replace incumbent party leadership across both chambers with aggressive, disciplined reformers committed to working-class economic transformation and structural democracy defense.
* **Core Mandate:** Enforce caucus discipline on democracy, ethics, and economic baseline votes, stripping committee gavels and party funding from members who break ranks to protect corporate donors.
* **Platform Details:** See [**Post-Mortem Analysis & Electoral Strategy**](./2.%20Priority%20is%20to%20win%20back%20congress/1.%20Post%20mortem%20analysis.md) and [**Uniting the Reform Coalition**](./2.%20Priority%20is%20to%20win%20back%20congress/2.%20How%20to%20win.md).

## 3. Block and Neutralize Corrupt Supreme Court Judges

* **The Problem:** The captured 6–3 Supreme Court supermajority operates as an unelected veto council for billionaires and corporate interests, aggressively dismantling federal regulatory powers, civil rights, and democratic safeguards.
* **Immediate Action:**
  1. **Block Hostile Appointments:** Immediately halt and vote down all right-wing, Federalist Society judicial nominees across all federal appellate and district benches.
  2. **Statutory Jurisdiction Stripping:** Exercise Congress's explicit authority under Article III, Section 2 of the Constitution to strip the Supreme Court of jurisdiction over federal voting rights, civil rights, and healthcare protection statutes.
  3. **Judiciary Act & Court Expansion:** Pass legislation to expand the Supreme Court from 9 to 13–15 justices, establish 18-year term limits, and implement regularized appointments.
  4. **Enforceable Ethics Board:** Establish an independent, federally mandated judicial ethics board armed with subpoena and disqualification power.
* **Platform Details:** See [**Comprehensive Proposal to Reform the Supreme Court**](../4.%20Domestic%20Agenda/4.%20Political%20Reform/3.%20Judicial.md) and [**Big Changes: Court Expansion**](../2.%20Big%20Changes.md).

## 4. Activate Interstate Compacts (National Popular Vote & State Bulwarks)

* **The Problem:** The Electoral College repeatedly enables minority-rule presidents, while decentralized state legal systems leave citizens vulnerable to hostile out-of-state prosecutions and federal rollbacks.
* **Immediate Action:**
  1. **National Popular Vote Interstate Compact (NPVIC):** Fast-track state legislative passage in remaining target states to cross the 270 electoral vote threshold, ensuring the winner of the national popular vote automatically wins the presidency without a constitutional amendment.
  2. **Regional Interstate Defense Compacts:** Establish formal compacts between state governments to guarantee safe haven protections: refusing extradition, shielding medical records, protecting healthcare providers, and preventing out-of-state civil liability for reproductive and gender-affirming care.
  3. **Economic Bulwarks:** Coordinate interstate public banking, clean energy supply chains, and emergency healthcare purchasing consortia to insulate populations from federal economic coercion.
* **Platform Details:** See [**Create Bulwarks in Blue States**](./3.%20Create%20bulwarks%20in%20blue%20states%20(harm%20reduction).md), [**Executive Reform & Popular Vote**](../4.%20Domestic%20Agenda/4.%20Political%20Reform/2.%20Executive.md), and [**Big Changes: Popular Vote**](../2.%20Big%20Changes.md).

## 5. Strategic Sequencing: Strict Embargo on Abortion & Guns Without a Trifecta

* **The Principle:** It is **critically dangerous** to attempt federal legislative action on abortion or firearms without an established governing trifecta (White House, Senate majority, House majority), the abolition of the filibuster, and judicial neutralization.
* **The Trap:** Without a trifecta, introducing federal gun control or reproductive freedom statutes is political malpractice. It:
  1. Burns precious political capital on bills guaranteed to die in a divided Congress.
  2. Inflames single-issue reactionary mobilization, handing cultural wedges to right-wing opponents.
  3. Results in immediate judicial invalidation by a hostile Supreme Court, creating negative legal precedent that binds future generations.
* **The Doctrine:** Protect reproductive access and public safety at the state level through [**Blue State Bulwark Compacts**](./3.%20Create%20bulwarks%20in%20blue%20states%20(harm%20reduction).md). Keep federal legislative energy laser-focused on universal, kitchen-table economic benefits and structural democracy reforms until governing power is absolute.

## 6. Avoiding the "AI Regulation" Trap: Cognitive Liberty vs. Progressive Taxation

* **The Political Distraction:** Attempting to "regulate AI" at the algorithmic or code level is an inconsequential political distraction. It shares the **exact same cowlick as gun regulation**: nobody can enumerate a granular technical regulatory scheme that does not unconstitutionally trample on fundamental human rights.
* **Freedom of Thought & Expression:** 
  - Regulating artificial intelligence in the abstract is as nonsensical as attempting to regulate dreams or the wind.
  - Cognitive processing, generative synthesis, and software logic are extensions of the natural human right to **freedom of thought and expression** protected by the First Amendment. Censorious code auditing and government licensing of speech engines inevitably restrict basic rights in an unconstitutional manner.
* **The Real Solution — Progressive Taxation:**
  - The perceived harms of AI—wealth concentration, monopolistic extraction, and labor disruption—are not solved by policing algorithms. They are solved by an effective mechanism: **progressive taxation**.
  - **The Core Rule:** *You need AI to express your speech; you do not need to make tax-free corporate profits to express your speech.*
  - Tax compute infrastructure, tax mega-cap tech windfall profits, tax automated capital replacement, and channel those revenues directly into Universal Basic Income (UBI), public dividends, and worker retraining.

## 7. The Public Commons: Power, Water, and the "Village Well" Moral-Hazard Fee

* **Subsidized by the People, Not Capitalism:**
  - Water and electricity systems were built, subsidized, and guaranteed by the collective public. Utility pricing in America was never pure capitalism; it is a **common fee agreed upon by the community** solely to manage and maintain shared infrastructure.
  - The historical social compact was based on the principle of the **village well**: limits and costs were designed under the shared expectation that *no one man would draw 10x or 100x the water of the next man*.
* **No Inherent Corporate Right to Public Power:**
  - Having billions in capital does **not** grant a private tech conglomerate or hyper-scale data center the right to drain the people's water table or strain the regional electrical grid.
  - The money paid by consumers manages the infrastructure—it does *not* sell off the underlying right to the resource, which belongs eternally to the people.
* **The Non-Polluting Compromise:**
  - Corporations have the right to generate their own power—provided it is **100% non-polluting**.
  - Because tech conglomerates cannot currently operate their massive compute clusters without consuming public resources or burning fossil fuels, the public must impose a strict twofold requirement:
    1. **Moral-Hazard Utility Fee:** Commercial data centers and hyper-scale compute facilities drawing from the public grid or aquifers must pay a steep, escalating moral-hazard surcharge. This fee directly offsets and lowers residential household utility bills for working families.
    2. **Clean Community Surplus Mandate:** Big Tech must construct new, dedicated, non-polluting clean energy generation (geothermal, advanced nuclear, solar/wind storage) that not only powers 100% of their own operational load, but provides **surplus clean electricity to the surrounding local community for free**.
