# Strategic Roadmap: Winning and Governing

1. **How Movements Won in the Past:** Mass mobilization, disciplined party organization, and uncompromising defense of working-class interests.
2. **How to Win in Today's Media & Electoral Landscape:** Independent digital media networks, aggressive grassroots organizing, and direct confrontations with corporate oligarchy.
3. **Coalition Governance & Transforming the Party:** Unite the left parties, build a disciplined progressive voting bloc, and install reform-minded leadership.
4. **Governing Immediately Post-Sweep:** When the sweep is achieved, execute the [**Top Urgent Priorities Based on Election Sweep**](../4.%20Top%20Urgent%20Priorities%20Based%20on%20Election%20Sweep.md) (Ending the Filibuster, Replacing Leadership, Neutralizing/Expanding SCOTUS, and Interstate Compacts).
