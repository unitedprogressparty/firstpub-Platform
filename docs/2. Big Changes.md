# Big Structural Changes

1. **Progressive Taxes:** Wealth taxes on ultra-millionaires and closing corporate offshore tax loopholes.
2. **Carbon Tax & Green Energy Dividend:** Aggressive carbon pricing with direct dividend rebates to working families.
3. **Universal Basic Income (UBI):** Unconditional baseline economic security floor for every American.
4. **Popular Vote for President:** Abolish the Electoral College loophole via constitutional amendment or the [National Popular Vote Interstate Compact](./1.%20Preface%20End%20facism/4.%20Top%20Urgent%20Priorities%20Based%20on%20Election%20Sweep.md#4-activate-interstate-compacts-national-popular-vote--state-bulwarks).
5. **Expand Congress and Reform the Supreme Court:** Expand the House, rebalance the Senate, and [expand the Supreme Court to 13–15 seats with 18-year term limits](./4.%20Domestic%20Agenda/4.%20Political%20Reform/3.%20Judicial.md).
6. **Universal Healthcare:** Guaranteed, single-payer healthcare free at the point of service.
7. **Immediate Post-Election Priorities:** See our [**Top Urgent Priorities Based on Election Sweep**](./1.%20Preface%20End%20facism/4.%20Top%20Urgent%20Priorities%20Based%20on%20Election%20Sweep.md) (End the Filibuster, New Leadership, Block/Expand SCOTUS, Interstate Compacts).
